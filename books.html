Books

We’re building our first catalogue now.

Our first title is ready, with three more arriving in the coming months. Both will be available as properly produced paperbacks—printed on demand, so nothing sits in a warehouse waiting to be remaindered—and as ebooks, formatted for however you actually read: on the metro, before bed, in the gaps between everything else.

We’re not interested in rushing a catalogue into existence just to have one. Every title that appears here will have gone through the same process: a query that showed us a real voice, pages that backed it up, and an editorial process that respects a writer’s intentions rather than sanding them down.

If you’re a reader: check back soon. The first titles are close.

If you’re a writer: see what we’re looking for and send us your work.
